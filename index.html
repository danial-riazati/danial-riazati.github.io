01 About

Backend systems, shipped and kept running.

I am a software engineer with five years of production experience, mostly on the backend. My work has been the unglamorous, load-bearing kind: designing domain models and REST APIs, getting data access and caching right, wiring up payments and third-party providers, and making sure there is enough logging and health checking to understand what a system is doing at three in the morning.

At Eyvan Group, I led engineering for a live travel-booking platform, from backend architecture and API design through testing and containerized deployment. At Hasin Technology, I worked on high-throughput transaction processing and led development of Piki Smart Cart, an IoT retail prototype.

I like problems where the hard part is the system rather than the syntax: consistency across services, failure modes, observability, and the design decisions that make a codebase survivable two years later. I also maintain Avan, an open-source Persian calendar library for React published on npm.

02Open source · npm · 2025

Avan Persian Date Picker

A Jalali (Shamsi) calendar and date-range picker for React, published to npm.

Problem
Persian-calendar date pickers for React are typically unmaintained, bundle their own styling and handle RTL, holidays and range selection poorly — a recurring problem in every Iranian product I worked on.
Role
Author and maintainer.
Engineering
A TypeScript monorepo of four published packages: a headless core for Jalali conversion, calendar grids and digit handling; a React layer with calendar, single-date and range pickers built on custom hooks for dismissible layers and controllable open state; an Iran public-holiday dataset; and a CSS-token theme package. Built with pnpm workspaces and Turborepo, unit-tested with Vitest, released through Changesets.
Outcome
Published on npm as @avan-persian/react with a live demo built from the repository and deployed on every push.

  • coreJalali conversion, grid generation, digit handling
